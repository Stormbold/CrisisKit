# Safety Boundaries

CrisisKit is a **private/local preparedness tool**. It helps you prepare and publish offline emergency information for your own household, club, team or local group.

## What CrisisKit is

- A generator for offline-ready emergency pages
- A tool for documenting contacts, meeting points, checklists and documents
- A way to produce printable PDFs and pocket cards from structured YAML

## What CrisisKit is NOT

CrisisKit is **not**:

- An official warning app
- A disaster management control center
- Medical advice or dosage guidance
- Legal advice
- A prepper or panic app
- A platform for live hazard alerts
- A replacement for NINA, Cell Broadcast, fire department, police, rescue services or authorities

**NINA** is the federal warning app of Germany. CrisisKit must not replicate or replace it.

## Medication data

CrisisKit may document medications for private reference only. It must not recommend medications or calculate dosages.

## Public deployment

If you publish a generated site publicly, private contacts, addresses or medical notes may become visible.

Only publish after reviewing and removing sensitive information.

## Standard disclaimer

> CrisisKit is a private/local preparedness tool. It does not replace official warnings, emergency services, medical advice, or instructions from authorities.
